# Mobile UX: colours, bottom sheets, one list + Cards, Wrong label — plan (2026-11-16)

Status: proposed. Decisions D1–D9 follow Hugo's answers of 2026-11-14, D10 his answers of 2026-11-16, D11 his OK of
2026-11-17. Builders: read only your work package (§4) plus the decisions it names.

## 1. Goal

Make the iPhone Home Screen app comfortable: one meaning per colour, bottom sheets that never jump and always swipe
closed, inputs that stay above the keyboard, Sheets as the one list, with a List | Cards switch, and a full-screen Cards round that
replaces Quick and the Practise panel and never needs scrolling. Add a **Wrong** label, separate from the four statuses, set automatically by wrong Cards answers,
and an answer field on every Sheets row that turns green or red and leaves no trace (D10).

## 2. Decisions

**D1. Colour roles: one meaning per hue.** Tokens in `styles.css` §1 (light / dark):

| Role | Token(s) | Light | Dark | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Controls (ink) | `--accent`, `--accent-press`, `--accent-tint`, `--on-accent` | #000000, #3a3a3c, rgb(0 0 0 / 0.08), #ffffff | #ffffff, #d1d1d6, rgb(255 255 255 / 0.14), #000000 | primary buttons, active chips/segments, tab bar, nav-bar buttons, menu ticks |
| Links | `--link` (new) | `--label` + underline | same | inline links (ink text is not enough on its own) |
| Wrong | `--wrong`, `--wrong-tint` (new) | #c8213a, rgb(200 33 58 / 0.11) | #ff453a, rgb(255 69 58 / 0.18) | wrong verdicts, Again grade, leech tag, Wrong label, writing mistakes |
| Review grades Hard/Good/Easy, "almost" verdicts | `--fill`, `--label` | — | — | neutral; their words carry the meaning |
| Brand | `--brand` (new) | #c8213a | #ff453a | app icon only (no UI use required) |
| Mastered | `--green`, `--badge-green` | #1f7a36, #34c759 | #30d158 | status text / bar |
| Not known | `--yellow` (new), `--badge-yellow` (new), `--yellow-tint` | #7a5c00, #ffcc00, rgb(255 204 0 / 0.22) | #ffd60a, #ffd60a, rgb(255 214 10 / 0.18) | status text / bar (Excel yellow) |
| Studying | `--blue`, `--badge-blue` | #0a62c9, #007aff | #409cff, #0a84ff | status text / bar (the app already writes 8FAADC blue) |
| Not studied | `--label-2`, `--gray` | #6c6c70 | #98989f | no bar |
| Tones | `--t1` … `--t5` | see D2 | see D2 | pinyin text only |

Rules: `--accent` never means right/wrong; red appears only as `--wrong`; a disabled primary button is `--fill` with
`--label-3` text, never a faded accent. Text and icons on an `--accent` fill use `--on-accent`, never a hard-coded
white (ink flips to white in dark mode). Every Not known use moves from orange to the yellow tokens, with dark text on
a solid yellow fill. Review grades: Again = `--wrong`; Hard, Good and Easy become neutral (`--fill` + `--label`), told
apart by their words and intervals, because orange, green and blue now belong to tones and statuses; "almost right"
verdicts (`.verdict.tones`, `.verdict.near`) become neutral the same way. `--orange`/`--orange-tint` remain only as the
background of `.notice` panels (never next to pinyin). Keep the legacy aliases (`--seal`, `--amber`…) pointing at the new
roles so untouched screens keep working.

**D2. Tones stay coloured everywhere (Hugo, Q2), on hues no status or Wrong uses.** `Pinyin` (`common.tsx:90`) already
colours every syllable through `.t1`–`.t5`; only the token values change. Starting values, computed for this plan:

| Tone | Hue | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | orange | #b54a00 | #ff9f4d |
| 2 | cyan | #00778a | #4fd3e8 |
| 3 | violet | #7b3fd1 | #b794ff |
| 4 | magenta | #b8237a | #ff6eb4 |
| 5 | neutral | #6c6c70 | #98989f |

Checked: every tone ≥ 4.5:1 on `--bg-elev` and `--bg` in both themes; CIEDE2000 ΔE ≥ 15 between any tone 1–4 and every
status, `--wrong` and other tone colour (worst pairs: light 19.0 t4/wrong, dark 22.3 t3/t4). ΔE ≥ 20 is not reachable in
light mode with AA text (best search result 19.5), hence 15. Hugo approves the hues on the Batch 1 screenshots; the
hue-to-tone assignment may change, the thresholds may not.

**D3. One sheet component.** `panel.tsx` exports `Sheet` (the current `Shell` renamed and generalised; keep `Shell` as an
alias for one release of code, not for users). Props add `size: 'full' | 'content'`.
- `full` (word panel, scratch pad): fixed height `calc(100dvh - env(safe-area-inset-top) - 10px)` on phones, so changing
  content never moves the top edge (S1). Content scrolls inside.
- `content` (status, filter, More menus, the write-to-workbook dialog): sized by content, max 92dvh — their content does not
  change while open.
- Drag to close from anywhere in the sheet when `scrollTop === 0`, not only the header (S3). A touch that starts inside
  `.pad-cell`, `.sp-canvas`, `input[type=range]`, a horizontally scrolling row, or any `[data-sheet-nodrag]` element never
  starts a drag (S2) — matched by selector in `panel.tsx`, so the writing components need no change. Same thresholds as
  today (110 px, or 40 px at > 0.6 px/ms).
- While a sheet is open the page behind does not scroll (lock on `documentElement`, restored on close).
- Phones only (< 900 px); the desktop side panel and centred menus stay as they are.
- Keep every e2e hook: `role="dialog"` + `aria-label`, `role="menu"`/`menuitemradio`, the "Close" and "Cancel" buttons,
  and the word panel bottom flush with the viewport (`e2e/explain.py`).

**D4. Keyboard.** New `src/ui/keyboard.ts`: `useKeyboardInset()` listens to `visualViewport` resize/scroll and sets the
CSS variable `--kb` (px the keyboard covers: `innerHeight - vv.height - vv.offsetTop`, clamped ≥ 0) on
`documentElement`; returns the number. Sheets pad their bottom with `max(var(--kb), env(safe-area-inset-bottom))`; the
Cards dock sits at `bottom: var(--kb)`. `index.html` viewport gains `interactive-widget=resizes-content` (Chrome/Android;
iOS ignores it, the hook covers iOS). Mounted once in `App.tsx`.

**D5. Cards (Hugo, Q4; D11).** Cards is the drill, Sheets the only list (D11); the List | Cards switch sits in Sheets.
- Route `#/cards` = a Cards round on the focus shell (no tab bar, like Review). Close returns to `#/sheets`. Old `#/quick`
  links (bookmarks, the Home Screen app reopening on them) redirect to `#/sheets`.
- Deep link: `#/cards?sheet=<tab>&status=<filter>&at=<noteId>` builds the deck from that Sheets tab (or All) and filter
  and starts at that word. Read once, then `history.replaceState` to `#/cards`. The switch, the row pencil, the `W` key
  and the word panel's "Practise this word" open it.
- Layout, top to bottom: top bar (close · "7 / 20" · settings) · progress bar · deck caption ("HSK五级 · All · 18 words ·
  Pinyin") · card (flex: 1, centred) · answer dock fixed at the bottom above the keyboard (field, Show answer, Check;
  after Check, one Next button). Nothing to scroll (Q1).
- Answer with: Hanzi, Pinyin, French, Draw, chosen in the settings sheet with the layer chips, presets and Shuffle.
  Pinyin follows D10: digits alone are tones ("3"), letters are full pinyin ("shao3"), exactly as in the list.
- No spoilers: before Check, nothing on screen shows the answer layer (no title, no header, no aria-label).
- After Check: the hidden layer appears, tone-coloured; a verdict line (Right, or Wrong with what was expected); a wrong
  card shows its new Wrong tag on the card at once (D6). Return = Next.
- Draw: prompt = pinyin + meaning; `WritePad` sized `min(available width, available height)`; outline and reveal as icon
  buttons; after Check, `StrokeAnimation` shows the answer; verdicts per D7.
- A "Scratch" tool in the dock opens the scratch pad sheet in every answer type (replaces the "Free pad" mode).
- End of round: today's summary plus the words answered wrong, each with its Wrong tag; Retry wrong; Send to reviews.
- The word panel loses its Practise tab: Explain + status picker, plus "Practise this word" (opens the deep link).
- `sheets/Practice.tsx` (TypeMode, DrawMode, PracticeBody) is deleted; `ScratchPanel` moves to `sheets/ScratchPad.tsx`,
  the `.sp-*` rules to `sheets/scratch.css`.

**D6. Wrong label (Hugo, Q5/5a/5b).** Two new mark kinds in the existing marks stream (ENG-2): `flag:wrong` and
`unflag:wrong`. Per note, the latest of the two by (t, id) wins; the word is Wrong when that is `flag:wrong`.
- Set automatically by a wrong Cards answer (wrong typed answer, Show answer, or a wrong Draw, D7) — only if the word is
  not already Wrong, so a drill does not pile up marks. Review grades never set it (assumption; Hugo may extend later).
- Cleared only by Hugo: tap the tag on a row (Sheets, end of round) or "Clear Wrong" for the words in the
  current filter. Clearing writes `unflag:wrong` marks — never `undoMark`, which only removes this device's own marks.
- Separate from status: it never changes a status and a status change never clears it. Never written into Excel or the
  PC copy; included in backups (marks are backed up whole).
- Shown as a red "Wrong" tag (icon + word) beside the status chip, on Sheets rows and on the card; "Wrong" filter in the
  Sheets filter menu (it also builds Cards decks, D11).
- Compatibility, no format bump: `decodeMarks` keeps rows with unknown actions (`sync.ts:114–133`), `applyMark` ignores
  them (`schedule.ts`, `default:` branch), `latestMarks` and `markStatus` skip them (`schedule.ts:116`, `status.ts:44`),
  `checkMarks` in `backup.ts` accepts any string. An older app shows statuses unchanged; its Words history prints the raw
  kind (cosmetic). The engine golden snapshot must not change.

**D7. Draw verdicts.** Outline on = tracing: no verdict, nothing recorded, no Wrong label. Outline off: Right when the
word is finished with `hints === 0`; Wrong when revealed or `hints > 0`. Words without stroke data (`missing`) count as
neither.

**D8. Saved Quick sessions (QUICK-1 unchanged).** `CardSession` stays `v: 1`; a session saved by today's version still
loads. `Target` gains `'draw'`; `readTarget` already falls back to `'hanzi'` for unknown values, so the device-only pref
`hz.qm.answer` stays safe both ways. Typed answers still record no review events except "Send to reviews".

**D9. Records.** The lead adds to `docs/decisions.md`: UI-1 (colour roles, D1–D2), ENG-5 (flag marks, D6), QUICK-2 (Cards
replaces Quick and Practise, one list; D5, D11), and updates `src/ui/CLAUDE.md` (map, colour rule; the ios-design spec §5 red rule is superseded by D1).

**D10. Answer field in the list (Hugo, 2026-11-16).** Each Sheets row ends with an empty field: hanzi · pinyin · EN/FR ·
field · status. On phones the meaning and the field share the row's second line.
- The input kind is told apart automatically: any Han character → `checkHanzi`; digits only (spaces ignored) → tones, one
  digit per syllable, 1–4, with 5 or 0 for the neutral tone, compared with every accepted reading (`pinyinNum` and
  `altPinyin`, as `grade.ts` `targets` does); letters → `checkPinyin` (tone numbers or marks).
- Return, or leaving a field that holds text, checks it; Return then focuses the next row's field and scrolls it above
  the keyboard (D4 `--kb`). Editing the text clears the colour.
- Right = green tint, green border and ✓; anything else = `--wrong` tint, border and ✗ (`tones`/`near` are wrong, as in
  QUICK-1).
- No trace: no status, no Wrong label, no review event, nothing stored or synced. Answers live in Sheets' React state,
  by row key, until a reload or "Clear answers" (More menu); switching sheet tabs keeps them.
- Field: 16 px text, ≥ 44 px tall, `enterKeyHint="next"`, autocapitalise/autocorrect/spellcheck off,
  `aria-label="Answer for <hanzi>"`. Like the trail slot, the field never triggers the row's hold, peek or speak, and the
  row shortcuts (m k s u w e) ignore keys typed in it. Desktop: Tab and Return move between fields.

**D11. One list (Hugo, 2026-11-17: "list is perfect").** Sheets is the only list. It gains an "All" tab first (every
sheet; a word in several sheets shows once, at its first row), a List | Cards switch under the tabs (D5), and three
filters from Quick, same rules: Wrong (D6), Seen this week, Missed recently. The Quick tab and screen go; the tab bar is
Sheets · Settings. Cards keeps Quick's device prefs (`qm.answer`, `qm.seed`, the layer prefs). `sh.filter` is a per-device
Sheets pref (never synced), and `readStatus` already reads an unknown value as All, so older versions are safe.
