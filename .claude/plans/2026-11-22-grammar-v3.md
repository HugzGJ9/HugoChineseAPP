# Grammar window v3: tick-box filters, one language, tap any word

Status: skeleton (lead, 2026-11-22). Open points marked **OPEN** wait for Hugo. Builds on grammar-content (GRAM-1);
no stored-format or sync change.

## 1. What Hugo asked (2026-11-22)
1. Filters are not useful → tick boxes, multi-select.
2. Titles not right → group headers mix languages ("条件 · Conditions", "No family" in English under FR).
3. The 中/FR/EN switch must be rethought.
4. Explanations in one language only, chosen by Hugo.
5. In example sentences, tap any word (not only his own words) to get it explained.
6. Remove "Same family".

Answers: one language = the app-wide EN | FR (`store.lang`, the word panel's `LangSwitch`); rows read pattern big,
title below; filters = HSK level, Family, My status.

## 2. Decisions
- **D1 One language, app-wide.** Grammar reads `useApp(s => s.lang)`. The 中/FR/EN `Segmented` and `titleLang.ts` go;
  `LangSwitch` sits in the Grammar top bar. Titles, definitions, "when to use", register label, family names, sentence
  translations and section names all follow it. Nothing is shown twice. Fallback when the chosen language is missing:
  the other language with a small `EN`/`FR` tag (**OPEN Q1**).
- **D2 Rows.** Pattern (hanzi, large, `Formula`-style slots optional) on line 1; short title in `lang` on line 2;
  status icon left; HSK + family tags right (family name in `lang`).
- **D3 Filters replace grouping.** "Group by" goes. The list is one list in Excel order, so there are no group headers
  (this is what fixes issue 2). A "Filter" button opens a `Sheet` with three tick-box groups: HSK level (levels present
  only), Family, My status. Inside a group: OR; across groups: AND. Each box shows its count. Active filters show as
  removable chips under the search field, plus "n of N" and "Clear". Device-only state in localStorage `hz.gr.f`
  (like QUICK-1; never synced). Search still matches 中, FR and EN whatever `lang` is.
- **D4 Any word tappable.** Sentences are split with `Intl.Segmenter('zh', {granularity: 'word'})` (already used in
  `review/shared.tsx`, `import/build.ts`; per-character fallback), then Hugo's own words are laid over it by longest
  match (his words win where boundaries disagree). Three looks: his words underlined in status colour (as today);
  pattern words (from `note.hanzi` / alts) in bold ink; every other word plain but tappable (pressed state only).
  Punctuation not tappable.
- **D5 Panel for a word not in his list.** Same `WordPanel` in a dictionary-only mode: no "Your list" block, no status,
  no "Practise this word"; Dictionary, Claude "Explain more" and examples as today. A word in his list opens the normal
  panel.
- **D6** "Same family" section removed from the detail page.

## 3. Work packages (disjoint files)

### WP1 — grammar logic (engineer)
Files: `src/lib/grammar/list.ts`, `list.test.ts`, `types.ts`, new `filters.ts` + `filters.test.ts`; delete
`titleLang.ts`.
- `itemTitle(item, lang: Lang)` / `itemText(...)`: one language with fallback flag; drop `TitleLang`, `GroupBy`,
  `groupItems`, `itemSubtitle`.
- `filters.ts`: `GrammarFilter { hsk: HskLevel[]; family: (FamilyId|'none')[]; status: WordStatus[] }`,

  `filterCounts(items, f, statuses)` (count per box given the other groups).
- `sentenceTokens(zh, vocabIndex, pattern)` → `{ s, note?, pattern?, word }[]` per D4; replaces `segmentSentence`.
  Tests: overlay beats segmenter, pattern chars marked, punctuation `word: false`, no-Segmenter fallback.

### WP2 — Grammar screen (ui-dev)
Files: `src/ui/grammar/GrammarPage.tsx`, `grammar.css`.
- Top bar: `LangSwitch` instead of 中/FR/EN. List: search, Filter button (badge = active count), chips, rows per D2.
- Filter sheet per D3 (tick boxes are real `input[type=checkbox]` in labelled rows, iOS look per ios-design §3).

### WP3 — dictionary-only word panel (ui-dev)

- `WordPanel` accepts `note` **or** `word: string`; with `word`, builds a transient lookup target (no Note stored,
  nothing written) and hides status, Practise, "Your list". Pinyin in the title from the lexicon's first reading.

### WP4 — checks (qa, opus: batch touches src/lib)
`e2e/grammar.py` rewritten for v3: no 中/FR/EN switch; EN | FR flips titles, definitions and sentence translations
with nothing in the other language; filters (tick 2 HSK levels → union; + a family → intersection; survives reload;
Clear); tap a non-list word → dictionary panel without status; no "Same family"; desktop + iPhone 13, no sideways
scroll. Unit tests from WP1. `npm test`, `tsc`, build.

Order: WP1 ∥ WP3, then WP2, then WP4.
